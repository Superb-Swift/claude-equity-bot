## Defects found and fixed — 17

**16 of 17 surfaced from live runs against real filers. Self-tests caught 1.** Two of my own tests were defective: one vacuous (asserted "instant items have no YoY" and passed only because the fixture had a single entry), one pinning a bug as correct (asserted the MD&A extract should open with the orphaned tail of the section heading).

Grouped by root cause:

- **`companyfacts` is an append-only archive, not a current view.** Concepts a filer abandons persist forever. RTX rendered a 2020 gross profit and a 2024 debt balance as current; NVDA lost its revenue line entirely because the preferred concept fossilised at FY2022 while a live alternative sat unused. Fixed by a staleness rule (every line must end on the anchor date) plus concept selection by currency, not mere presence.
- **Caches masking correct fixes — three times.** Rendered-HTML Form 4s served for raw-XML requests; `_cache_path` collapsing directories via `basename`; the derived `fin_*.json` keyed on an accession that doesn't change when the extractor does. Fixed by read-time validation, full-path keys, and `EXTRACT_SCHEMA`.
- **Values the tool did not compute.** `net direction: mixed` from 0 buys / 0 sells; RSU grants (code A) counted as purchases, which would have read as "buying" every vesting quarter at MSFT/WMT/JPM; percent change across zero (CORN's +$30.4M from −$3.1M rendered as −1077.9%).
- **Mixed or misleading presentation.** Statement lines from different periods in one block (operating income exceeding gross profit); XBRL lagging the filing with no note; XBRL viewer renderings used as 8-K bodies; silent truncation at 8 lines while the tag said 9.
- **Extraction quality.** MD&A landing on forward-looking boilerplate, tables of contents, or mid-sentence; 8-K exhibit fallback missing "Exhibit **No.** 99".

**Two self-inflicted regressions**, both surfacing as *absences* rather than errors: a TOC guard that deleted DOW's genuine net-sales table, and a strict duration match that would have removed operating cash flow for every YTD-only filer.
